Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the application "miniar" (hereinafter referred to as "the App").

1. Advertisement Serving

The App uses a third-party ad serving service (Google AdMob). Ad serving providers may use cookies or device identifiers to display advertisements tailored to user interests.

These data are collected anonymously and do not personally identify individuals.

2. Information Collected and Data Handling

The App handles text, images, and audio data entered and registered as diary entries. All of these data are stored entirely within the user's device or in their own Google Drive, and are not collected or managed on servers operated by the developer.

Additionally, you can use device lock features (such as biometrics or passcodes) to protect your privacy and data within the device.

When using the backup feature, data is saved directly to your own Google Drive (app-specific folder).

3. Data Deletion

Users can delete backup data from the settings menu within the App. If deletion by yourself is difficult due to reasons such as having already uninstalled the App, you can delete the data on Google Drive by following these steps:

  1. Open Google Drive Settings.
  2. Select "Manage Apps".
  3. Click "Options" for "miniar" and select "Delete hidden app data".

Please note that the developer cannot directly manipulate or delete data stored in the user's Google Drive.

4. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated as necessary. The most current version of the policy will be posted on this page.
