Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application "todoni" (hereinafter referred to as the "App").

1. Advertisement Distribution

The App uses third-party advertising services (Google AdMob). Ad network providers may use cookies and device identifiers to display advertisements tailored to user interests.

This data is collected anonymously and does not personally identify individuals.

2. Acquisition of Personal Information

The App does not collect or store personal information (such as names, email addresses, or physical addresses) of users. When using the backup feature, data is stored in your own Google Drive (App Folder).

3. Data Deletion

Users can delete their backup data from the settings menu within the App. If deletion by yourself is difficult due to uninstallation or other reasons, you can delete the data on Google Drive by following the steps below:

  1. Open Google Drive Settings.
  2. Select "Manage Apps".
  3. Click "Options" next to "todoni" and select "Delete hidden app data".

Please note that the developer cannot directly access, manipulate, or delete data stored in your Google Drive.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Policy may be updated as necessary. The latest version of the privacy policy will always be posted on this page.
