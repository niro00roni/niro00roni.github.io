Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the application "kuratine" (hereinafter referred to as the "App").

1. Advertisement Distribution

The App uses a third-party ad delivery service (Google AdMob). Ad delivery operators may use cookies or device identifiers to display advertisements tailored to users' interests.

This data is collected anonymously and does not personally identify individuals.

2. Collection of Personal Information

The App does not collect or store users' personal information (such as name, email address, or physical address). When using the backup feature, data is stored in the user's own Google Drive (app-specific folder).

3. Data Deletion

Users can delete backup data from the settings menu within the App. If it is difficult for you to delete the data yourself due to reasons such as having already uninstalled the App, you can delete the data on Google Drive by following the steps below:

  1. Open Google Drive Settings.
  2. Select "Manage apps".
  3. Click "Options" next to "kuratine" and select "Delete hidden app data".

Please note that developers cannot directly access, manipulate, or delete data stored in the user's Google Drive.

4. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated as necessary. The latest policy will be posted on this page.
